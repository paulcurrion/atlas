Ngũgĩ wa Thiong’o, *Decolonising the Mind: The Politics of Language in African Literature* (1986): “The bullet was the means of the physical subjugation. Language was the means of the spiritual subjugation.” Ngũgĩ gave up English to write in Gĩkũyũ.
