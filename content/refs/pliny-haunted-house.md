---
title: Pliny haunted house
---

The earliest recorded haunted house is in Pliny the Younger’s letter to Sura (Letters 7.27), the story of the philosopher Athenodorus and a ghost chained in a house in Athens.

"For even in the day time, though the spectre did not appear, yet the remembrance of it made such a strong impression on their imaginations that it still seemed before their eyes, and their terror remained when the cause of it was gone. By this means the house was at last deserted, as being judged by everybody to be absolutely uninhabitable; so that it was now entirely abandoned to the ghost."
