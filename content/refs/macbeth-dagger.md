---
title: "Macbeth’s dagger"
tags:
  - literature
---

Shakespeare, *Macbeth* (Act II, scene 1): “Is this a dagger which I see before me, / The handle toward my hand?”, the vision of “a dagger of the mind” Macbeth sees before murdering Duncan, which ends “Hear it not, Duncan, for it is a knell / That summons thee to heaven or to hell.”
