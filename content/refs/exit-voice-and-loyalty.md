“Once you have exited, you have lost the opportunity to use voice, but not vice versa; in some situations, exit will therefore be a reaction of last resort after voice has failed.”

- Albert O. Hirschman, from Exit, Voice and Loyalty (1970). Members of a declining institution can leave (exit) or protest (voice), and loyalty shapes which they choose.
