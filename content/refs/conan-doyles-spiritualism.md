---
title: Conan Doyle's Spiritualism
---

Sir Arthur Conan Doyle, creator of the detective Sherlock Holmes, was also a keen spiritualist. Two of the talks he gave were recorded and printed on shellac. In the talks he explained his belief that "The basis of all religions is that we live after death and the proving of the truth of this basis is to my mind the most essential work in this age of perplexity and materialism, where the good and earnest man honestly doubt that the death is not the end."
