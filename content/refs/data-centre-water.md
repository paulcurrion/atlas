---
title: Data centre water
---

"Global data center capacity is expanding at an unprecedented rate... Direct water demand is rising in parallel: data centers currently consume 313–764 billion liters per year, (2) with projections suggesting that this could more than double to 1200 billion liters by 2030, (3) equivalent to the annual household and municipal water withdrawals of a country like The Netherlands. This rapid expansion illustrates how Artificial Intelligence (AI), though virtual in its services, is dependent on the use of natural resources, particularly energy and water."

- Lorenzo Rosa, Matteo Sangiorgio, Bridget R. Scanlon, Marco Mazzotti, Joseph Berry, Anna M. Michalak; One Quarter of Global Data Centers are Located in Water-Stressed Areas. Environ. Sci. Technol. 6 October 2026; 60 (39): 27780–27791.
